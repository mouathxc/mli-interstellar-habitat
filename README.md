## Overview

This project explores the thermal management of an interstellar habitat through numerical modelling and thermal analysis, with particular emphasis on radiative heat transfer and Multi-Layer Insulation (MLI).

The objective is to investigate thermal behaviour in a space environment, understand radiative energy exchange, and explore insulation strategies that help maintain stable internal temperatures while minimizing heat transfer.

## Engineering Scope

- **Radiative Heat Transfer:** Modelling thermal radiation exchange between habitat surfaces and the surrounding space environment.
- **Multi-Layer Insulation (MLI):** Investigating multilayer insulation as a passive thermal-control solution.
- **Thermal Management:** Exploring temperature distribution and heat-transfer mechanisms within the habitat.
- **Space Environment:** Considering the challenges associated with spacecraft thermal regulation.

## Methodology

The project follows a numerical modelling approach:

1. Define the habitat geometry and thermal boundaries.
2. Establish material properties and surface emissivity.
3. Model radiative heat-transfer mechanisms.
4. Investigate the thermal insulation configuration.
5. Analyse temperature distributions and thermal behaviour.
6. Evaluate the effectiveness of the proposed thermal-management approach.
